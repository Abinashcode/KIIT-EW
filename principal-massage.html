Principal’s Message

Prof.(Dr.) Tribikram Sarangi

Keeping this in mind, the mission of KIIT HSS Entrance Wings concerned to the organization is to provide positive catalytic impulse to every aspiring learner undergoing JEET and NEET Examination. It is vital to empower them all the necessities to solve the crisis. Untold motivation and close monitoring by experienced faculty members are milestones that bring a real growth in days come. In this rich platform the budding young learners manifest their aesthetic sensibilities in various genres of science.

​We are committed to nurturing the next generation who will light up the world with their passion and contribution by virtue of their new identification.

We do not prepare student for something, we help students to prepare themselves for anything

Principal
