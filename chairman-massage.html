Chairman’s Message

Dr. C.R Mishra

In an era defined by constant change and digital based learning Higher Education can no longer static. KIIT Higher Secondary Entrance Wings (JEE/NEET) stands at the fore front of the transformation reimagining technical careers by seamlessly blending academic excellence, scientific approach with a global outlook. Through our unique journey “Moring to Night” monitoring system we establish that every learner is guided, watched and mentored every single minute round the clock in a holistic boundary.

​We don’t’ judge complete the syllabus; we walk the path with our students, following they remain focused, disciplined, and morally strong to reach zenith.
